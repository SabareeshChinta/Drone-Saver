## 2. Unsupported / Excluded Failure Modes

The following failure modes are **EXCLUDED** from direct telemetry injection because they require physical transducers not present in standard glass cockpit telemetry:

1. **High-Frequency Knock Sensor Raw Accelerometry ($> 10\ \text{kHz}$ acoustic window):** Excluded because Garmin G1000 logs at 1 Hz; Detonation is instead detected via its macroscopic thermodynamic signature ($\Delta T_{\text{CHT}} \uparrow$, $\Delta T_{\text{EGT}} \downarrow$).
2. **Crankcase Blow-by Pressure ($P_{\text{crank}}$):** Excluded because crankcase pressure sensors are not installed on standard general aviation airframes; modeled indirectly via oil temperature and power loss.
3. **In-Cylinder Pressure Transducer Piezoelectric Traces ($P(\theta)$ vs crank angle):** Excluded because production aero-engines do not have combustion chamber pressure transducers.
